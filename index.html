Atlanta, Georgia

Welcome to Atlanta

Atlanta is the capital of the state of Georgia. The city is famous for its booming job market, influential music and film scenes, deep civil rights history, diverse food, and major travel connectivity. As the birthplace of trap music and a hotspot for hip-hop and R&B, Atlanta offers tons of opportunities to catch live shows at venues like Tabernacle or Terminal West. It is the home of big name companies such as Coca-Cola, The Home Depot and Delta Airlines.
